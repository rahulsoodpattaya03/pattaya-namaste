Namaste Pattaya is a family of travellers and locals who go out together safely and respectfully. Treat everyone the way you want your sister, mother or friend to be treated.

Not allowed (we remove it and may close your account)

  2. Anyone under 20, or content involving minors in any way. We report this to the police immediately.
  3. Harassment, threats, stalking, unwanted sexual messages, or hate speech about religion, caste, race, nationality, gender or sexuality.
  4. Sharing private or intimate photos, or anyone's personal details (phone, hotel room, address) without permission.
  5. Illegal drugs, weapons, gambling, fake documents, scams, money requests or investment offers.
  6. Fake profiles, pretending to be someone else, or using someone else's photos.
  7. Content that breaks Thai law, including content about the monarchy, national security or public order that is illegal in Thailand.
  8. Spam, advertising or sending people to other apps for money.

Ladies lounge

  • Only verified women can join. Men are not allowed, and trying to get in by pretending is a permanent ban.
  • What is said in the lounge stays in the lounge. Screenshots shared outside are a serious breach.
  • Moderators may remove posts that put anyone at risk.

Staying safe

  • Meet in public places first. Tell a friend where you are going.
  • Never send money to someone you met in the App.
  • Watch your drink. Never drink and drive.
  • Use Report and Block. Reports are private.
  • Emergency: Tourist Police 1155, Police 191, Ambulance 1669.

What we do

  • We review reports, normally within 24 hours.

  • 20+ only. Thai law forbids selling alcohol to anyone under 20. Venues check ID.
